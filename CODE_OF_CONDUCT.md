# Code of conduct

This project is used by people who depend on text-to-speech, including
screen-reader users. Please keep every interaction respectful and
constructive.

- Be welcoming to newcomers and to people with different abilities,
  backgrounds and levels of experience.
- Criticise ideas and code, never people.
- Assume good faith; ask before concluding.
- No harassment, insults, discriminatory language or unwanted personal
  attention, in any project space.

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the maintainer through the
contact details on [github.com/mahdi-shafiei](https://github.com/mahdi-shafiei).
Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation of
anyone who does not follow this code.
